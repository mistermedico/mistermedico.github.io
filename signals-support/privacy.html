Privacy Policy — English

1. App publisher

The publisher of Signals! Team Card Game is Nitai Shore.

2. Information the app collects

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It requires no account and does not request a name, email address, phone number, or profile.

3. Local gameplay data

Gameplay progress, settings, achievements, daily streaks, statistics, and unlocks are stored only on the device. The publisher does not receive this information and provides no developer-operated cloud sync for it.

4. Permissions, tracking, and analytics

The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health information, or advertising tracking. It contains no analytics, telemetry, or third-party SDK that collects data.

5. Network use, advertising, and purchases

Version 1.0 works offline, contains no advertising SDK, and makes no advertising request. It is free and offers no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or purchase restoration. Development-only mock code that is unavailable to users cannot show ads or perform tracking.

6. Deletion and device backups

Deleting the app removes its locally stored gameplay data, subject to device backups controlled by the device owner or operating-system provider. Because there is no account or developer server, the publisher cannot restore deleted progress.

7. Future changes

If a future release adds a feature that changes how information is handled—including analytics, advertising, purchases, an account, or sync—this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that release is distributed. The effective date above will also be revised.

8. Support-site privacy

These support pages contain no form, cookies, analytics, or JavaScript. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical access logs, such as IP address and browser type, under its own policy; that information is not received through the app's code.
