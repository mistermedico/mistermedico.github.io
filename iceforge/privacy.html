Privacy & support

Updated 30 October 2026 · Version 3.0

Your game stays on your device

ICEFORGE runs hockey matches locally. Coaching notes, training completion, selected club, match results, control preferences, practice bests and saved tactics are stored in your browser or app’s local storage. These records are not sent to an ICEFORGE server. Recent goal replay frames are held only in memory during the current session. There are no analytics, advertising SDKs, in-app purchases or tracking technologies in the app code. Fonts and game assets are bundled.

Website hosting

The web version is hosted through ChatGPT Sites. Its hosting and sign-in provider may process technical request data and account information under its own privacy terms. This public privacy/support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process technical request logs under its own terms. This is separate from the bundled iOS app, which plays without website sign-in or a network connection. The iOS app has not yet been submitted to Apple.

Clearing your data

Clear this site’s browser storage to delete local progress. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local data, subject to your device backup settings. Uninstalling may cause progress to be lost; there is no cloud recovery.

Permissions and children

The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library access. Sound is optional and starts only when enabled. There is no chat, user-generated public content or account registration in the bundled app.

Support

For game issues or privacy questions: nitaishor@gmail.com. If you email support, your email provider processes that communication; include only details needed to help with the issue.

Simulation limits

This is an independent arcade hockey game with fictional clubs. It is not affiliated with professional leagues or national federations. Coaching drills are general game content.
