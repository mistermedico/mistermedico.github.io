ICEFORGE Hockey Studio

Support for the independent hockey arcade and coaching game.

Contact support

Email nitaishor@gmail.com with your app version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, signing keys or other private account credentials.

Controls

Use the virtual joystick to skate. Pass to combine with teammates; tap a teammate for a direct pass. Hold Shoot and release to fire. Set shot direction with Shot aim. Deke evades a challenger; Check dislodges a nearby opponent’s puck. Open Controls in the match studio for instructions.

Saved progress

Tactics, training progress, notes and match results are saved on your device. There is no cloud synchronisation or account recovery. Clearing app or browser storage removes progress.

Privacy

Read the ICEFORGE privacy policy. The app code has no advertising, in-app purchases or tracking SDKs.
