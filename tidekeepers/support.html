How to play

Choose a tool and a ground tile. You have two actions per turn. Water spreads one orthogonal tile per turn. A seed grows into a permanent forest after two wet turns. Pumps protect a five-tile cross for three turns. Meet both the home and forest goals before the last turn.

Progress and offline play

Progress is local to your device. Deleting the app removes it. Gameplay works offline; purchases and optional ads need a connection.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings → Full Journey and purchases → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. For billing or refund requests, visit Apple’s purchase support.
