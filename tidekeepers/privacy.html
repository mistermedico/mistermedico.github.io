Tidekeepers privacy policy

Developer: NITAI SHORE. Contact: amiretproinfo@gmail.com.

Local gameplay

The native iPhone and iPad game stores progress and preferences on your device. No game account or gameplay server is required. Deleting the app removes the local save. Results are shared only when you use the system share action.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions to unlock and restore the Full Journey. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Optional advertising

Additional tactical hints may be earned by choosing to watch a Google AdMob rewarded advertisement. The app requests non-personalized ads. Ad and consent services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, consent and fraud prevention. Non-personalized does not mean anonymous. Consent and privacy choices are presented where required; the privacy-options action is available in Settings when required.

See Google’s partner-site data policy. Declining or being unable to load an ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay or retrying a map.

Contact and choices

You can remove local progress by deleting the app. To ask about this policy, contact the developer above. This policy describes the native app; a separately hosted web demo may have its own hosting and font behavior.
